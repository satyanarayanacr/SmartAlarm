[ ] 4.1 Confirmation ON vs OFF
Create:

* 7:00 AM, Repeat Mon-Fri
* Confirmation ON (9:00 PM)
Alarm B:
* 7:30 AM, Repeat Mon-Fri
* Confirmation OFF
Expected:

* Confirmation flow excludes Alarm B.
* Both alarms remain independently scheduled.

[ ] 4.2 Different Days
Create:
Alarm A:
* Monday only, 7:00 AM
* Confirmation 9:00 PM (Sunday night)
Alarm B:
* Wednesday only, 7:30 AM
* Confirmation 8:00 PM (Tuesday night)
Expected:
* Each alarm's confirmation is calculated against its own next occurrence.
* Sunday night's confirmation only asks about Monday's Alarm A.
* Tuesday night's confirmation only asks about Wednesday's Alarm B.

[ ] 4.3 Midnight Boundary
Test:
Alarm:
* 12:15 AM (e.g. Thursday morning)
* Confirmation: 11:00 PM (Wednesday night)
Expected:
* Confirmation occurs on the preceding calendar day (Wednesday 11:00 PM).
* Correct occurrence is associated with the confirmation.
* No accidental confirmation is generated for the wrong day.

[ ] 4.4 Confirmation Time After Alarm Time
Test:
Alarm:
* 7:00 AM
* Confirmation: 8:00 AM
Observed Behavior & Specification Logic:
* ConfirmationTimeCalculator.confirmationTriggerForOccurrence:
  - If confirmation time on occurrence date is strictly before the alarm: fires same day.
  - If confirmation time is AT or AFTER the alarm time (8:00 AM is after 7:00 AM):
    fires the day BEFORE the occurrence at 8:00 AM (23 hours before the alarm).
* Outcome: The confirmation event fires 23 hours ahead (the morning before the alarm).
  This mathematically guarantees the confirmation is never triggered after an alarm has already rung.
