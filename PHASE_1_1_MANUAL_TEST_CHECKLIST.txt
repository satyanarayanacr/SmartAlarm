SMART ALARM - PHASE 1.1 - MANUAL TEST CHECKLIST
To be run by you, on a real device, and checked off by you.
I have not run any of these and am not claiming any of them pass - only
what is described as verified in PHASE_1_1_ANDROID_REPORT.txt (which, at
the time this checklist was written, is nothing yet - no real build has
happened for Phase 1.1).

Before starting, make sure Phase 1's existing manual tests
(PHASE_1_MANUAL_TEST_CHECKLIST.txt) still pass - Phase 1.1 must not have
broken any of that.

[ ] A. Open Settings from the alarm list. Confirm "Daily alarm
       confirmation" appears, defaults to enabled, with a default
       confirmation time of 9:00 PM.

[ ] B. Change the confirmation time (e.g. to a couple of minutes from
       now, for testing) and leave the screen. Re-open Settings and
       confirm the new time is still shown (persisted).

[ ] C. Create at least one WEEKLY alarm that includes tomorrow's day of
       the week. Also create a ONE_TIME alarm for some time tomorrow.

[ ] D. Set the confirmation time to a couple of minutes in the future
       and wait for it. Confirm a "Tomorrow's alarms" notification
       appears, and that its count reflects only the WEEKLY alarm(s),
       not the ONE_TIME alarm.

[ ] E. Tap "Keep All" on that notification. Confirm the notification is
       dismissed and every alarm still rings normally at its scheduled
       time tomorrow (nothing was changed).

[ ] F. Trigger the confirmation again (next day or by resetting the
       time). Tap "Review" this time. Confirm the app opens directly to
       a review screen listing tomorrow's WEEKLY alarms, each with a
       checkbox, all checked by default.

[ ] G. On the review screen, uncheck one alarm and leave the rest
       checked, then tap "Skip Selected". Confirm only the checked
       alarms are skipped for tomorrow, and the unchecked one remains
       scheduled (matches the spec's example: e.g. 7:00 AM SKIPPED,
       8:30 AM KEPT, 6:00 PM KEPT).

[ ] H. Confirm the alarm you skipped in step G does NOT ring tomorrow.

[ ] I. Confirm the alarm you skipped in step G DOES still ring on its
       next matching day after tomorrow (i.e. the recurring rule itself
       was not disabled - only that one day was skipped).

[ ] J. Confirm the alarm(s) you kept in step G still ring normally
       tomorrow at their scheduled times.

[ ] K. With multiple WEEKLY alarms scheduled for tomorrow, open the
       review screen and tap "Skip All". Confirm every one of them is
       skipped for tomorrow and none of the parent alarms are disabled
       in the alarm list.

[ ] L. Let a confirmation notification appear and simply ignore it (do
       not tap anything). Confirm that when the alarm's actual time
       arrives tomorrow, it still rings normally - "no response" must
       never silently skip an alarm.

[ ] M. Create a WEEKLY alarm, then disable "Daily alarm confirmation" in
       Settings. Confirm no confirmation notification appears at the
       configured time while it is disabled, and that the alarm still
       rings normally on its own schedule.

[ ] N. Re-enable "Daily alarm confirmation" in Settings. Confirm the
       daily confirmation notification resumes at the configured time.

[ ] O. Reboot the device with "Daily alarm confirmation" enabled and at
       least one WEEKLY alarm scheduled. Confirm the confirmation
       notification still appears at the configured time after reboot
       (tests that the confirmation event, not just alarm occurrences,
       survives reboot).

[ ] P. Skip an occurrence for tomorrow (as in step G), then reboot the
       device before tomorrow arrives. Confirm that occurrence is still
       shown as skipped after reboot and does NOT ring - i.e. reboot
       recovery must not resurrect a skipped occurrence.

[ ] Q. On the review screen, tap "Skip Selected" twice quickly (or tap
       it, then try again) on the same alarm. Confirm there is no crash
       and no unexpected duplicate behavior (idempotent skip).

[ ] R. If your device lets you deny notification permission: deny it,
       then confirm the app does not crash, the confirmation feature
       simply produces no visible notification, and all alarms
       (including WEEKLY ones) continue to ring normally as scheduled.

[ ] S. Confirm the app still works correctly on both a phone-sized
       layout and, if available, a tablet or landscape wide layout - the
       new Settings and Review screens should appear as a second pane
       in the wide layout, matching how the existing alarm editor
       behaves, not as a separate implementation.

[ ] T. Re-run a few of Phase 1's original manual checks (creating,
       editing, deleting a plain alarm; snooze; dismiss; a ONE_TIME
       alarm ringing and auto-disabling) to confirm nothing about
       ordinary Phase 1 alarm behavior regressed.
