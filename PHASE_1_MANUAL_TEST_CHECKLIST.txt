SMART ALARM - PHASE 1 - MANUAL TEST CHECKLIST
To be run by you, on a real device, and checked off by you.
I have not run these and am not claiming any of them pass - only what's
in PHASE_1_ANDROID_REPORT.txt (build/test/lint/one confirmed alarm ring)
is verified so far.

[ ] 1.  Create a ONE_TIME alarm a few minutes in the future. Confirm it
        appears in the alarm list with the correct time.
[ ] 2.  Confirm that alarm rings at the exact scheduled time, with the
        full-screen ringing UI showing over the lock screen.
[ ] 3.  Confirm the ONE_TIME alarm auto-disables itself after it fires
        (toggle shows off, no new occurrence is scheduled).
[ ] 4.  Create a WEEKLY alarm for one or more days of the week. Confirm
        it schedules for the correct next matching day/time.
[ ] 5.  Let a WEEKLY alarm fire once. Confirm it stays enabled and a new
        occurrence is scheduled for the following matching day.
[ ] 6.  While an alarm is ringing, tap Snooze. Confirm the ringing UI
        dismisses and the alarm rings again after the snooze interval.
[ ] 7.  While an alarm is ringing, tap Dismiss. Confirm the ringing UI
        and notification both clear and the alarm does not ring again
        until its next real occurrence.
[ ] 8.  Edit an existing alarm's time/days/name. Confirm the change is
        saved and the next ring reflects the new schedule (old scheduled
        occurrence does not also fire).
[ ] 9.  Delete an alarm. Confirm it disappears from the list and does
        not ring at its old scheduled time.
[ ] 10. Toggle an alarm off, then back on. Confirm it does not ring
        while off, and resumes correctly once re-enabled.
[ ] 11. Create two alarms scheduled close together (a minute or two
        apart). Confirm both ring independently and neither is dropped
        or overwrites the other's notification.
[ ] 12. Reboot the device with at least one alarm still scheduled in the
        future. Confirm it still rings correctly after reboot (tests
        BootReceiver/RescheduleAllUseCase).
[ ] 13. Reboot the device after intentionally missing an alarm's time
        while it was off (device off through the scheduled time).
        Confirm: a ONE_TIME alarm shows as missed/auto-disabled and does
        NOT fire retroactively; a WEEKLY alarm shows as missed for that
        occurrence but is still enabled with its next occurrence
        correctly scheduled.
[ ] 14. On Android 13+, revoke notification permission for the app from
        system Settings, then let an alarm's scheduled time pass. Confirm
        (and note) the known Phase 1 limitation: no ringing UI/sound
        appears for that occurrence (see report section 3, item 2) -
        this is expected, not a bug to report.
[ ] 15. Resize/rotate to a wide-screen or tablet-like layout (or use a
        large-screen emulator/foldable if available). Confirm the
        two-pane list+edit layout renders without overlapping elements,
        especially around the areas that were reworked to avoid
        Modifier.weight() (see report section 3, item 1).
[ ] 16. Force-close the app entirely (swipe away from recents) with an
        alarm still scheduled. Confirm the alarm still rings at its
        scheduled time (AlarmManager-based alarms must survive the app
        process being killed, not just backgrounded).
